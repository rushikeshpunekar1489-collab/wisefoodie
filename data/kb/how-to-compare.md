# Comparing Packaged Foods Side-by-Side

The WiseFoodies comparison tool (`/compare`) allows consumers to compare **2 to 4 products** side-by-side to understand nutritional differences, ingredient trade-offs, and economic value.

### How to Use the Compare Tool
1. **Navigate to `/compare`**: Search and select 2, 3, or 4 products across our 1,130+ item catalog.
2. **From Product Reports**: Click the **Compare** button on any product card or report page (`/food/[slug]`).
3. **From Alternatives Carousel**: When viewing "Better Alternatives" on any product, click "Compare Side-by-Side" to load both items into the matrix instantly.
4. **From Favourites**: In `/favourites`, check any 2 to 4 products and tap "Compare Selected".

### What the Comparison Matrix Evaluates
- **Food Benefit Score (FBS)**: Compare final scores and color-coded score bands.
- **Nutrition Comparison (per 100g)**: Side-by-side bars for Energy, Protein, Added Sugar, Saturated Fat, Sodium, and Fibre.
- **Value Intelligence**: Price, pack size, price per 100g, and Protein Per Rupee (g / ₹1).
- **Ingredients & Additives**: First two primary ingredients, presence of palm oil vs desi ghee/mustard oil, whole grains vs refined maida, and flagged industrial additives.
- **Automated Verdict**: Highlights which product delivers higher protein, lower added sugar, cleaner ingredients, or superior value per rupee.

### Same-Category Recommendation
For meaningful comparisons, compare products within the **same category** (e.g., comparing one biscuit to another, or one breakfast cereal to another). While the matrix permits cross-category comparisons (e.g., milk vs potato chips), the chatbot and comparison engine will clarify that cross-category items serve fundamentally different dietary roles.

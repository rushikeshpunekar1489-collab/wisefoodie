# Exploring and Searching the WiseFoodies Catalog

WiseFoodies provides multi-dimensional browsing and search tools across our 1,130+ packaged products via `/explore` and `/search`.

### Filtering by Category and Subcategory
Browse items grouped into standard packaged food categories:
- **Dairy & Frozen**: Fresh milk, curd, paneer, butter, cheese, ice cream.
- **Biscuits & Cookies**: Glucose biscuits, cream biscuits, digestive cookies, rusks.
- **Snacks & Savouries**: Potato chips, extruded snacks, bhujia, roasted namkeens, makhana.
- **Beverages**: Fruit juices, soft drinks, tea, coffee, energy drinks, malted drinks.
- **Noodles & Sauces**: Instant noodles, pasta, ketchup, mayonnaise, pasta sauces.
- **Breakfast Cereals**: Corn flakes, muesli, rolled oats, granola.
- **Chocolates & Confectionery**: Milk chocolate, dark chocolate, candy, gummies.
- **Ready To Cook & Eat**: Ready curries, instant mixes, poha, upma.

### Ingredient-Based Filters ("Contains" vs "Free From")
Under `/explore`, use dynamic ingredient chips to isolate specific components:
- **"Free From (No ...)"**: Filter products without Palm Oil, Refined Flour (Maida), Added Sugar, Synthetic Colours, or Artificial Preservatives.
  - *Strict Verification Rule*: Products are only labeled "Free From" if their ingredient list is completely verified. If ingredient data is unverified or missing, products are isolated in an "Ingredients Unknown" drawer.
- **"Contains"**: Find products formulated with Whole Grains, Real Milk, Desi Ghee, Nuts & Seeds, or High Protein pulses.

### Smart Sorting Options
- **FBS Score: High to Low**: Discovers the highest-rated nutritional formulations first.
- **Protein: High to Low**: Ranks by protein per 100g.
- **Sugar: Low to High**: Surfaces foods with minimal or zero added sugars.
- **Sodium: Low to High**: Ideal for lower-salt diets.
- **Nutrition Per Paisa**: Ranks by grams of protein delivered per ₹1.
- **Price: Low to High**: Ranks by absolute retail price (MRP).

### Natural Language Search Router
The search bar (`/search`) accepts natural language intent phrases:
- `"high protein biscuit under 50"`
- `"without palm oil snacks"`
- `"low sugar breakfast cereal"`
- `"amul vs mother dairy"`
- `"maggi noodles score"`

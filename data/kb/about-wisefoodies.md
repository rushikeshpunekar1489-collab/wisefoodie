# About WiseFoodies

WiseFoodies is an independent Indian packaged food intelligence platform designed to decode packaged food labels, nutritional declarations, and ingredient lists into clear, actionable health and value metrics.

### Our Core Mission
Packaged food labels in India are frequently difficult to interpret, displaying deceptive serving sizes, hidden added sugars, industrial additives, and marketing claims like "High Protein" or "Real Fruit" that mislead consumers. WiseFoodies provides scientific, non-judgmental, transparent food analysis so individuals and families can make informed choices.

### Key Capabilities
1. **Catalog of 1,130+ Packaged Products**: Across dairy, biscuits, breakfast cereals, snacks, beverages, noodles, sauces, chocolates, and ready-to-eat foods.
2. **Food Benefit Score (FBS)**: An algorithmic score on a 1.0 to 10.0 scale reflecting overall nutritional density, protein, fibre, added sugars, sodium, trans fat, and processing level.
3. **Nutrition Per Paisa**: An economic value metric computing protein and dietary fibre delivered per ₹1 and per ₹10 spent.
4. **Better Alternatives ("Isse Behtar Option")**: Instant healthy swaps within the same product category.
5. **Smart Cart & Food Diary**: Real-time basket macro analysis and daily ICMR-NIN nutrition tracking.
6. **Barcode Scanner & OCR**: Fast on-device camera lookup and label text recognition.
7. **Ask WiseFoodies AI Assistant**: Instant retrieval-grounded guidance trained on our exact catalog and food methodology.

# ICMR-NIN Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDA)

WiseFoodies benchmarks all packaged food nutritional declarations against the scientific guidelines established by the **ICMR-NIN (Indian Council of Medical Research – National Institute of Nutrition, 2020)** for sedentary to moderate Indian adults.

### Daily Reference Baselines (2000 kcal Diet)

| Nutrient | Daily Reference Value | Benchmark Type | Context & Health Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy** | **2,000 kcal** | Caloric Baseline | Reference energy intake for standard adult maintenance. |
| **Protein** | **54 g** | Minimum Target | ~0.83g per kg body weight; builds muscle, enzymes, and immune cells. |
| **Dietary Fibre** | **30 g** | Minimum Target | Soluble & insoluble fibre for gut microbiome and glycemic control. |
| **Added Sugar** | **25 g** max | Upper Limit | WHO/ICMR upper threshold (<5-10% total caloric intake). |
| **Sodium** | **2,000 mg** max | Upper Limit | Equivalent to 5g of table salt (NaCl) daily. |
| **Saturated Fat** | **20 – 22 g** max | Upper Limit | Recommended upper limit (<10% total energy intake). |
| **Total Fat** | **65 g** | Balance Range | Healthy fats including MUFAs and PUFAs. |
| **Total Carbs** | **275 g** | Balance Range | Complex carbohydrates from whole grains, pulses, and vegetables. |

### Visual RDA Percentage Bars
On every product report page (`/food/[slug]`), WiseFoodies renders horizontal progress bars indicating what percentage of your daily allowance a 100g portion (or single serving) represents.
- **Harmful / Limit Nutrients (Sugar, Sodium, Saturated Fat)**:
  - `< 20% RDA`: Green (Leaf) — within safe single-meal thresholds.
  - `20% – 40% RDA`: Yellow (Mango) — moderate load; exercise portion control.
  - `> 40% RDA`: Red (Tomato) — heavy load for a single packaged item.
- **Beneficial Nutrients (Protein, Fibre)**:
  - Higher percentages indicate superior nutritional density per calorie.

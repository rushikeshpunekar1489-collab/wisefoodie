# Whole Grains and Traditional Indian Millets

Unlike stripped refined flours, whole grains retain their complete anatomical structure, offering sustained satiety, stable energy release, and essential micronutrients.

### Key Whole Grains in Indian Diets
1. **Whole Wheat (Atta)**: Traditional stone-ground chakki atta retains the germ and bran layer, providing natural B-vitamins and fibre.
2. **Rolled & Steel-Cut Oats**: Rich in **beta-glucan**, a soluble dietary fibre scientifically proven to assist in lowering blood LDL cholesterol.
3. **Finger Millet (Ragi / Nachni)**: Outstanding plant-based calcium source (approx. 340mg calcium per 100g — more than 3x that of milk), plus slow-release carbohydrates.
4. **Sorghum (Jowar) & Pearl Millet (Bajra)**: Naturally gluten-free ancient grains high in iron, magnesium, and resistant starch.
5. **Pulses & Legumes (Chana, Moong, Dal)**: High-protein whole-food staples delivering both complex carbs and balanced amino acids.

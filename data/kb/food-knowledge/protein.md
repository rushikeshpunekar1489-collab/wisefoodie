# True Protein Density vs Marketing Hype

Many packaged snacks in India advertise "Power Protein" or "High Protein" on their packaging while delivering very little meaningful protein relative to their calories and price.

### Evaluating Real Protein Quality
1. **Check Protein per 100 kcal**: If a biscuit provides 4g of protein per 100g but packs 480 kcal and 32g of sugar, the protein density is poor. You are consuming massive calories to receive negligible protein.
2. **Examine the Protein Source**:
   - High-quality complete proteins: Milk solids, whey, paneer, curd, eggs, soy protein isolate.
   - Plant proteins: Whole pulses (moong, chana, urad), nuts (almonds, peanuts), and seeds (chia, pumpkin).
   - Low-quality fillers: Wheat gluten added to refined maida to artificially inflate protein numbers.

### Daily Dietary Requirements
- **ICMR-NIN RDA**: Recommended daily protein intake for standard Indian adults is **54 grams** (~0.83g per kg body weight). Active or athletic individuals may require 1.2g to 1.6g per kg.
- **Nutrition Per Paisa**: Check the protein per rupee metric on WiseFoodies to see if you are paying for real protein or premium marketing.

# The Serving Size Manipulation Trap

One of the most widespread label deception tactics in the packaged food industry is the manipulation of declared **serving sizes**.

### How the Trap Operates
- A 100g pack of cookies or potato chips may declare a serving size of **"15g" (approx. 1.5 cookies)**.
- In the "Per Serving" column, calories might look modest (e.g., 75 kcal) and added sugar appears minimal (e.g., 4g).
- In reality, an average consumer eats half the pack or the entire 100g pack in a single sitting, unknowingly consuming **500 kcal and 28g of sugar** (7x what they thought).

### The Golden Rule: Compare per 100g
To protect shoppers, WiseFoodies standardizes all evaluations on a **per 100g (or 100ml)** basis. This eliminates serving size games and allows fair, direct comparisons between brands.

# Refined Wheat Flour (Maida) vs Whole Grains

A whole grain of wheat consists of three distinct anatomical parts:
1. **Bran**: The rough outer protective shell containing over 80% of the grain's dietary fibre, B vitamins, and trace minerals.
2. **Germ**: The nutrient-rich reproductive core packing healthy fats, Vitamin E, and antioxidants.
3. **Endosperm**: The large starchy middle layer consisting almost entirely of simple carbohydrates.

### What Is Maida?
**Maida (Refined Wheat Flour)** is produced by stripping away both the nutrient-dense bran and the germ, leaving only the starchy endosperm.
- **Fast Digestion**: Without fibre to slow gastric emptying, maida digests almost as quickly as pure sugar, resulting in high blood glucose and insulin spikes.
- **Low Nutrient Density**: It loses 70% to 80% of its intrinsic vitamins, minerals, and dietary fibre during milling.

### The "Brown Bread" Marketing Trap
Many commercial "Brown Breads" and "Atta Noodles" sold in India contain 70% to 90% refined maida, given a brown appearance using synthetic or ammonia caramel colour (INS 150d) or malt extract. Always verify that **"Whole Wheat Flour" or "Atta"** is declared as the first ingredient on the back label.

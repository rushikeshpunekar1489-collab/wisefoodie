# Dietary Fibre and Gut Health

Dietary fibre consists of non-digestible carbohydrates from plants that play essential roles in digestion, glycemic regulation, and cardiovascular health.

### Soluble vs Insoluble Fibre
1. **Soluble Fibre (e.g. Beta-glucan in Oats, Pectin in Fruits)**:
   - Dissolves in water to form a viscous gel in the digestive tract.
   - Slows glucose absorption, dampening sudden blood sugar spikes.
   - Binds to dietary cholesterol and bile acids, supporting healthy blood lipid profiles.
2. **Insoluble Fibre (e.g. Bran in Whole Wheat, Cellulose in Vegetables)**:
   - Adds bulk to stools, accelerating transit time and promoting intestinal regularity.
   - Acts as prebiotic nourishment for beneficial gut microflora.

### Daily Recommendations and Benchmarks
- **ICMR-NIN Guideline**: Recommended intake is **30 grams of dietary fibre daily** for adults.
- Most packaged Indian snacks provide under 2g of fibre per 100g because refined flours (maida) have had their bran layer removed.
- Look for packaged foods that provide at least **6 grams of fibre per 100g**.

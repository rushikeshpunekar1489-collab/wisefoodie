# Reading Indian Nutrition Labels

Front-of-pack marketing claims like "Natural", "Fit", "100% Whole Wheat", or "Immunity Booster" in India often conceal high sugar, refined palm oil, and high sodium.

### The Rule of Descending Order
By law under FSSAI regulations, ingredients must be declared in descending order of weight at the time of manufacture.
- The **first 2 to 3 ingredients** make up the primary bulk of the product.
- If **sugar, liquid glucose, palm oil, or refined wheat flour (maida)** appear in the top 3 spots, the food is essentially an ultra-processed confection or fried carb, regardless of front-of-pack claims.

### Per 100g vs Per Serving
Always evaluate products using the **Per 100g (or 100ml)** column rather than the per-serving column.
- Brands frequently define absurdly tiny serving sizes (e.g. 15g of chocolate or 2 biscuits) to make harmful nutrients look safe.
- Comparing everything on a 100g standardized basis reveals the true concentration of sugar, sodium, protein, and fat.

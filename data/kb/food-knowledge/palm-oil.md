# Edible Vegetable Oil and Palm Oil Realities

When you see the generic phrase **"Edible Vegetable Oil"** on an Indian snack or biscuit package, it almost universally refers to **refined palm oil or palmolein**.

### Why Manufacturers Rely on Palm Oil
1. **Low Cost**: Palm oil is the cheapest edible vegetable oil produced globally.
2. **Semi-Solid at Room Temperature**: High saturated fat content gives biscuits crispness and chocolate coatings a smooth melt without expensive dairy butter.
3. **High Smoke Point**: Extreme thermal stability allows commercial snack fryers to reuse oil over multiple industrial frying cycles.

### Health Implications
- **High Saturated Fat**: Palm oil contains approximately **45% to 50% saturated fat** (predominantly palmitic acid), which raises LDL cholesterol when consumed in large daily quantities.
- **Industrial Processing & Free Radicals**: Repeatedly heating palmolein in commercial snack production creates oxidized lipid compounds.
- **Healthier Alternatives**: Look for snacks prepared with cold-pressed mustard oil, groundnut (peanut) oil, sunflower oil, or pure desi cow ghee.

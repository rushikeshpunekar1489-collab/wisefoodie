# Hidden Sodium in Savoury Foods and Staples

While table salt (Sodium Chloride) is the most obvious source of sodium, processed packaged foods contain massive hidden sodium loads through preservatives, stabilizers, and flavour enhancers.

### Converting Between Salt and Sodium
- **Formula**: $\text{Sodium (mg)} \times 2.5 = \text{Salt (mg)}$.
- 1 gram of table salt contains approximately **400 mg of elemental sodium**.
- **ICMR-NIN & WHO Upper Limit**: Adults should consume no more than **2,000 mg of sodium per day** (equivalent to 5g or 1 flat teaspoon of salt).

### Common Hidden Sodium Sources in Packaged Foods
1. **Instant Noodles & Seasoning Tastemakers**: A single 70g pack can contain 800mg to 1,200mg of sodium — over 50% of the entire daily allowance in one light meal.
2. **Packaged Breads & Baked Goods**: Baking soda (Sodium Bicarbonate, INS 500ii) adds significant sodium even in sweet or plain bread loaves.
3. **Flavour Enhancers**: Monosodium Glutamate (MSG, INS 621) and Disodium Ribonucleotides (INS 635).
4. **Preservatives**: Sodium Benzoate (INS 211) and Sodium Metabisulphite (INS 223) in sauces and fruit juices.

### WiseFoodies Sodium Benchmarks (per 100g)
- **Low Sodium**: $\le 120\text{ mg}$ per 100g.
- **Moderate Sodium**: $120\text{ mg}$ to $600\text{ mg}$ per 100g.
- **High Sodium**: $600\text{ mg}$ to $800\text{ mg}$ per 100g.
- **Excessive Sodium**: $> 800\text{ mg}$ per 100g (incurs extra FBS penalty).

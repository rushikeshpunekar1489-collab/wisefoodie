# Using the Barcode and Label Scanner

The WiseFoodies Scanner (`/scanner`) enables instant in-store product lookup using your phone's camera, image upload, or manual barcode entry.

### Three Ways to Scan
1. **Live Camera Scan**: Point your smartphone camera directly at the packaging barcode or front of pack. Uses rear-facing camera stream (`facingMode: environment`).
2. **Photo Upload**: Upload a photo of the product front-of-pack or nutritional table from your device gallery.
3. **Manual Barcode Search**: Type the 8, 12, or 13-digit EAN/UPC barcode printed beneath the black-and-white lines (e.g., `8901058852394`).

### Tips for Best Scanning Results
- **Avoid Glare and Reflections**: Shiny plastic packaging (such as chips or biscuit wrappers) reflects grocery store lights. Tilt the pack 15 degrees away from overhead lights.
- **Keep Flat**: Smooth out crinkled pouches so barcode lines are parallel and straight.
- **Ensure Sufficient Lighting**: Avoid casting a shadow over the barcode with your phone.

### What Happens If a Product Is Not in the Catalog?
If a scanned barcode is not yet in our 1,130+ product database:
1. The scanner displays: *"Product not found in WiseFoodies database yet."*
2. Offers a 1-click **Report Missing Product** modal where you can submit the product name, brand, and photo.
3. Provides quick links to search for similar products in the category or explore verified alternatives.

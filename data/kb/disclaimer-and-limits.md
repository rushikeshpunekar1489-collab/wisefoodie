# Platform Disclaimers and Limitations

### Informational Purpose Only — Not Medical Advice
The **Food Benefit Score (FBS)**, nutritional breakdowns, Nutrition Per Paisa calculations, and AI assistant replies on WiseFoodies are provided strictly for general consumer informational and educational purposes.
- **WiseFoodies is NOT a medical organization, clinical dietitian, or healthcare provider.**
- Nothing on this platform constitutes medical diagnosis, dietary prescription, treatment, cure, prevention, detox, or clinical management of any health condition.
- Individuals with medical conditions (including Type 1 or Type 2 Diabetes, hypertension, kidney disease, cardiovascular illness, or metabolic disorders) must consult a qualified physician or registered clinical dietitian before making dietary changes.

### Essential Mandatory Health Ending
All health-related queries addressed by the WiseFoodies platform and AI assistant must conclude with the standard disclaimer:
> *"FBS is an informational score based on label data, not medical advice."*

### Allergen Cross-Contact Disclaimer
Packaged food ingredient lists display declared recipe ingredients; however, commercial industrial facilities frequently process multiple items on shared manufacturing equipment.
- Manufacturing lines may involve cross-contact with allergens including **peanuts, tree nuts, gluten (wheat), milk, soy, sesame, eggs, or sulphites**.
- Individuals with severe food allergies must always inspect physical packaging for allergen advisory warnings (e.g., *"Manufactured in a facility that also processes tree nuts"*).

### Price and Packaging Variations
Retail prices (MRP), packaging formats, and ingredient formulations may vary regionally across different states in India or across batch manufacturing dates. WiseFoodies reflects the label information recorded from sampled retail batches.

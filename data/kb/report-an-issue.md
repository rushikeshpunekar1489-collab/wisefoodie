# Reporting Data Issues and Missing Products

Accuracy is fundamental to WiseFoodies. If a manufacturer changes packaging, alters pricing, reformulates ingredients, or if you spot an error on any product page, you can report it directly.

### How to Report an Issue on an Existing Product
1. On any product report page (`/food/[slug]`), click the **"Report an issue"** button located at the bottom of the nutrition report.
2. Select the issue type:
   - **Wrong or outdated price (MRP)**
   - **Nutritional values don't match the pack**
   - **Ingredients have changed or are missing**
   - **Product image is incorrect or outdated**
   - **Barcode mismatch**
3. Add a brief description and optionally upload a photograph of the physical back-of-pack label.
4. Submit the report. Our review queue (`data/review-queue.md`) logs the item for human verification.

### How to Request a Missing Product
If a packaged food you regularly purchase in India is not yet in our 1,130+ catalog:
1. Open the Scanner (`/scanner`) and scan the barcode.
2. When the scanner reports *"Product not found in WiseFoodies database yet"*, tap **Report Missing Product**.
3. Provide the brand name, product name, and an optional front/back packaging photo.
4. Items with high consumer request counts are prioritized for physical sourcing, OCR transcription, and scoring in our next catalog update.
